## ✅ Prerequisites

To qualify for ISSAP certification:

- Hold a valid **CISSP certification**.
- Have **two years of full-time experience** in at least one domain from the ISSAP Exam Outline.

Alternatively:

- Have **seven years of cumulative experience** in at least two ISSAP domains.
- Educational qualifications (bachelor’s/master’s degree in IT or related fields) or additional ISC2 credentials may count towards experience.
